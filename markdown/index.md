DOs also have a different philosophy than the MDs, that the body is inherently able to heal itself. Sometimes it just needs a little help from us. 

We Believe in preventative medicine versus The MDs Who learn about the organs and their illnesses and how to treat those illnesses after the fact. We study that, plus how illness in one area of the body effects other areas of the body, how all the connective tissues in the body interact and effect the rest of the body. We have extra training that involves multiple alternative ways to prevent, treat and maintain health in the body including the use of vitamins and supplements. Our goal is whole body wellness. 

Psychiatry is based on symptoms that the brain produces when there is an illness. They do studies in many patients with the same illness to find a list of the most common symptoms. They then use that list as criteria to diagnose each mental illnesses. Those lists do not take into consideration where the illness may be coming from. Those symptoms can be created by many different illnesses. There are many new studies now that show much of mental illness is actually hormonal imbalances and that psychiatric treatment is only treating the symptoms and not the cause. There are genetic mutations and vitamin deficiencies that cause psychiatric symptoms. there are other medical illnesses, including infections that cause psychiatric symptoms.

Dr. Stone believes in finding the root cause of the psychiatric symptoms and eradicating that with the long-term goal of returning the brain to optimum health. Most psychiatrists focus on treating the symptoms and continuing medication indefinitely. Covering up the symptoms so the brain can function better, is not the answer. Let's fix why we have the symptoms and eradicate them. Thus our brain can work to its full potential.

while medication may need to be used initially for stabilization the overall goal is to get the brain making its own chemicals so that we can be on the least/ smallest amount possible or even no medication at all.
